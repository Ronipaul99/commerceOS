# M00.01 — Merchant process discovery

**Status** Historical commerce-pilot draft. ADR-025 moved foundation discovery to the [SaaS workspace journey](M00.01-saas-workspace-discovery.md). The examples below are reference material for a later commerce pilot.

**Source** [Master blueprint §§3 and 5](../MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md#3-users-org-model-roles-and-top-level-workflows) and [SC-01 through SC-06](../MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md#24-end-to-end-reference-acceptance-scenarios).

Tenant A represents a fashion/imitation jewellery pilot and Tenant B a saree pilot. The current workflows below are scenarios to walk through with merchants. The proposed workflows reflect the v3 baseline. Each scenario uses the selected merchant and branch throughout, including its inventory location and register where applicable.

## Tenant A · Fashion jewellery

### Current workflow to validate

| Stage | Buyer or supplier | Owner or social seller | Stock, dispatch, or finance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Enquiry and hold | Buyer asks about a piece available during a live sale. | Seller identifies the exact piece and promises a temporary hold. Check who records the promise and how another seller finds it. | Staff verify the final piece is physically available. Check how overlapping holds are resolved. |
| A2. Sale and collection | Buyer accepts the quote and offers a partial advance. | Seller records the order and the person credited with the sale. Check when a hold is promoted to an order. | An authorized person checks the collection evidence and tracks the remaining due. Check how a receipt and dispatch approval are handled. |
| A3. Dispatch and return | Buyer receives the piece or requests a return or exchange. | Owner checks eligibility and communicates the outcome. | Staff pack and track dispatch, inspect any return, and record the refund, replacement, or stock disposition. Check how damage and failed delivery are recorded. |
| A4. Replenishment | Supplier delivers some of the ordered pieces. | Owner follows up on outstanding units and differences on the supplier bill. | Staff count and inspect the delivery. Check how rejected pieces and supplier balances are tracked. |

### Proposed BusinessOS workflow

| Stage | Owner or social seller | Stock or dispatch | Finance or approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Set up and book | Owner resumes setup, configures the branch and location, previews opening stock, and invites staff. Seller makes a manual booking for a known SKU. | One active reservation can hold the final sellable unit. The second seller sees a conflict. | Owner confirms the applicable collection, discount, invoice, and returns settings before the validation sale. |
| A2. Convert and collect | Seller converts the booking with the buyer and salesperson attribution attached. The current hold is carried into the order allocation. | Picker sees only orders for an authorized branch and location. The hold expiry retry leaves the consumed reservation unchanged. | Authorized verifier records the partial advance. The order shows remaining due and proceeds only under the relevant merchant policy. |
| A3. Fulfil and resolve | Seller checks the order timeline and follows the applicable cancellation or return workflow. | Staff pick, pack, dispatch, and post the issue once. Inspect a returned piece and quarantine damage before resolution. | Approver authorizes the eligible refund, credit, or exchange. Any replacement is a linked order and financial adjustments reference the original. |
| A4. Replenish | Purchaser raises a PO for approved quantities. | Receiver posts accepted pieces to sellable stock, isolates damaged pieces, and leaves the remainder open. | Reviewer compares PO, receipts, and bill. Supplier payable and matching are reviewed where that capability applies. |

## Tenant B · Sarees

### Current workflow to validate

| Stage | Buyer or supplier | Owner or cashier | Stock, dispatch, or finance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1. Quote and availability | Buyer asks for two specific fabric, weave, or colour variants. | Seller checks each variant and shares a quote. Check whether a quote alone promises stock and when a missing item is escalated. | Staff check the shelf or warehouse and confirm which variant can be picked. |
| B2. Counter sale and close | Walk-in buys a saree and proposes cash plus UPI. | Cashier records both tenders, issues the appropriate sales document, and counts cash at shift close. Check how discount, void, expense, and refunds are authorized. | Reviewer compares the counted drawer with the sales and cash records. Check where variances and UPI evidence are kept. |
| B3. Receiving and dispatch | Supplier ships a partial delivery. Buyer asks for delivery or pickup. | Owner reconciles missing or damaged quantities and decides when the open order can proceed. | Receiver inspects stock. Staff pick variants separately and record packages and delivery outcomes. |
| B4. Exchange or failed delivery | Buyer asks to exchange a variant, or a carrier returns an undelivered shipment. | Seller checks the original purchase and passes the request to the approver. | Staff inspect the item on return. Check how stock, store credit or collection, and any COD remittance are reconciled. |

### Proposed BusinessOS workflow

| Stage | Owner, seller, or cashier | Stock or dispatch | Finance or approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1. Set up and quote | Owner sets up the branch, stock location, and register. Seller links a two-line quote to an enquiry and converts it when both variants are available. | One unavailable variant blocks confirmation under the pilot default. A quotation holds stock only through an explicit reservation. | Server revalidates price and tax configuration on conversion. An authorized override requires a recorded reason. |
| B2. Counter sale and close | Cashier opens a branch register, scans the SKU, records mixed tender, and issues the configured sales document. | POS uses the same stock and order authority as social and manual sales. | Finance distinguishes the cash drawer from UPI evidence. At close, the manager reviews any variance with its reason and the linked cash movements. |
| B3. Receive and fulfil | Purchaser creates an approved PO. Seller confirms the order when accepted goods are available. | Receiver separates sellable and quarantined stock, leaves partial receipt open, and records remaining quantities later. Staff pick, pack, and dispatch split packages. | Finance reviews a supplier bill mismatch and any eligible partial settlement against the supporting documents. |
| B4. Resolve exchange or RTO | Seller links the approved exchange to the original line. Failed delivery follows a separate return-to-origin workflow. | Staff record parcel return and inspection. Only an eligible restock disposition adds to sellable inventory. | Approver reconciles any extra collection, store credit, or refund. COD remittance remains outstanding until verified separately. |

## Actors and screens to review

Responsibilities below describe working roles for the walkthrough. The same person may perform several roles, subject to the approved permissions and separation of duties.

| Actor | Responsibility and scope | Screens or evidence to walk through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tenant owner or business admin | Set business policies and locations and invite staff for their merchant. | Sign-in and workspace, setup wizard, business settings, import preview and errors, activation checklist, staff invite. |
| Social seller or sales agent | Record enquiries, quotes, bookings, and orders in an assigned branch. Capture who sold the item. | Customer lookup, enquiry and quote, live session queue and hold timer, manual order, order detail and timeline. |
| Cashier | Operate an assigned branch register and record tenders and cash counts. | Register open, scan and cart, discount approval, mixed tender, receipt, return lookup, close. |
| Inventory, fulfilment, or receiving staff | Verify stock and condition, record receipts, and fulfil lines at assigned locations. | Product and SKU, branch stock and holds, pick list, package and shipment, goods receipt, return receipt and inspection. |
| Purchaser | Manage supplier and PO details within the permitted purchasing scope. | Suppliers, draft and approved PO, partially received quantities, bill variance where enabled. |
| Branch manager or finance reviewer | Review authorized exceptions, payments, refunds, and cash variances within their scope. | Approval queue, collection verification, balances and COD reconciliation, cashbook, return decision, invoice and credit documents where enabled. |
| Buyer, supplier, or carrier | Provide the relevant request, delivery, or payment evidence through the merchant’s workflow. | Walk through the evidence hand-off and the record the internal actor uses to follow up. |

### Worked examples for merchant review

Use these inputs to discuss the expected records and handoffs. Amounts are synthetic final totals in paise. Each example starts from its own opening balances. Run through both tenant perspectives, using the same local SKU alias `SKU-001` where helpful. Record what the merchant would expect to see for their branch and which other branches or tenants should be excluded.

| Scenario | Walkthrough input | Outcome to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SC-01 | Preview 100 rows containing one duplicate SKU and one invalid opening quantity. Leave and resume setup before committing. | The preview reports 98 valid and 2 rejected rows. After an explicit valid-only commit, compare the committed counts and opening stock before the validation sale. Confirm which setup steps require approval. |
| SC-02 | `SKU-001` has one sellable unit at the selected location. Two sellers request the hold. The winning buyer owes 120,000 and provides a verified advance of 45,000. | One hold succeeds and available stock becomes zero while on-hand remains one. Conversion uses the same unit and leaves 75,000 due. An expiry retry leaves the converted allocation intact. Confirm who can verify the advance and authorize fulfilment. |
| SC-03 | Request one unit each of `SKU-001` and `SKU-002`. Start with one unit of the first and zero of the second. Receive one `SKU-002` later and dispatch in two packages. | Confirmation waits for the missing unit. After dispatch of the first package, one unit remains to be shipped. After the second, zero remain. Confirm how each package and its stock movement is linked to the order. |
| SC-04 | Open with cash of 100,000. On a sale with an approved discount already reflected in the 175,000 total, collect 75,000 cash and 100,000 verified UPI. Record a 10,000 cash expense and a 25,000 approved cash refund. Count 139,000 at close. | Cash expected is 140,000. Counted minus expected is −1,000, so the reviewer should see a 1,000 shortage. UPI remains separate from the cash drawer. Confirm who can approve the refund and how the variance is reviewed. |
| SC-05 | Order 10 units and receive 6, including 1 damaged. Receive the remaining 4 in good condition. A supplier bill differs from the PO. | The first receipt adds 5 sellable units and 1 quarantined unit, with 4 still due. The second makes the totals 9 sellable, 1 quarantined, and 0 due. Confirm how the mismatch is reviewed and how an approved partial payment is evidenced where payables apply. |
| SC-06 | From a delivered two-line order, return one damaged item with an eligible credit of 120,000. The linked replacement totals 150,000. Separately, a failed shipment with a 90,000 COD amount enters RTO. | The damaged return adds zero sellable units. Using the approved credit against the replacement leaves 30,000 to collect. The separate RTO does not establish COD collection or settlement. Confirm the required evidence at return inspection and financial resolution. |
