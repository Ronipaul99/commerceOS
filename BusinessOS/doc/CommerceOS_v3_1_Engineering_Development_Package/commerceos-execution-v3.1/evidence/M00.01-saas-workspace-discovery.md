# M00.01 — SaaS workspace discovery

**Status** Design ready for Product and Architecture review. The workspace examples use synthetic data. Review and RG0 signoff are pending.

**Authority** [ADR-025 and WS-01 through WS-03](../MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md#235-adr-025-general-purpose-saas-foundation), [workspace setup requirements](../MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md#5-business-onboarding-and-optional-capability-setup-p0), and the [M00.01 roadmap entry](../ENGINEERING_EXECUTION_ROADMAP.md#m0001--saas-workspace-discovery).

## Workspace journey

| Step | Owner or teammate action | Expected workspace behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Verify identity | The owner registers and completes the verification flow selected under G-01. | The verified identity can proceed to workspace creation. For a failed or expired verification, show the recovery or retry action. |
| Create and resume | The verified owner creates a private workspace draft, saves its name, locale, and time zone, then signs out and returns. | Restore the draft and saved settings only for the authorized owner. Highlight missing or invalid core settings. |
| Activate | The owner reviews the basic workspace settings and activates it with optional modules disabled and invitations deferred. | Activate the workspace after validating ownership and core settings. Show the empty workspace home, settings, and team access. |
| Invite and manage access | The owner invites a teammate to a workspace role. The teammate accepts the invitation. The owner later revokes that access. | A valid invitation grants only the chosen role. Expired and reused invitations are rejected. Revocation takes effect for existing access and is recorded in the workspace audit history. |
| Switch workspace | A user who belongs to both workspaces selects the other one. A teammate who belongs to only one tries to open the other workspace's settings. | Switch only for a verified active membership. Load data and actions allowed by the selected workspace's grants. Deny access to the other workspace for the single-workspace teammate. |

## Actors and screens

| Actor | Scope | Screens for the foundation demo |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Own workspace setup, settings, member invitations, and permitted role changes. | Sign-up, verification, workspace setup, home, settings, team and invitations, workspace switcher, and audit history. |
| Administrator | Workspace administration actions explicitly granted to the member. | Workspace home and granted settings or team actions. |
| Teammate | Workspace and actions granted by active membership. | Invitation acceptance, sign-in, home, permitted settings, and access denied states. |
| Platform operator | Restricted platform operations under separate operator permissions. | Tenant metadata and approved support records. Owner and teammate access use their own workspace memberships. |

## Synthetic tenants and acceptance cases

Use these synthetic workspaces. Both have no branches and all optional modules disabled. Each records its own settings, members, and audit history.

| Workspace | Display name | Locale | Time zone | Verified owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `WORKSPACE-A` | Workspace Alpha | `en-IN` | `Asia/Kolkata` | `OWNER-A` |
| `WORKSPACE-B` | Workspace Beta | `bn-IN` | `Etc/UTC` | `OWNER-B` |

| Identity | `WORKSPACE-A` access | `WORKSPACE-B` access | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OWNER-A` | Owner | Member | Verify that a user's owner permissions in A do not carry over to B. |
| `OWNER-B` | None | Owner | Verify B's settings and audit history independently. |
| `MEMBER-A` | Member after accepting an invitation in WS-02 | None | Verify invited access, revocation, and isolation for a single-workspace user. |
| `MEMBER-B` | None | Member | Keep an independent teammate in B for isolation checks. |

For WS-02, start `MEMBER-A` without a membership. Replay the same invitation after acceptance and use a different expired invitation for the expiry case. For WS-03's single-membership checks, start with an accepted `MEMBER-A` membership and reset the fixture separately from WS-02's revocation case.

| Case | Evidence expected at implementation | Backlog trace |
|---|---|---|
| WS-01 | The owner saves a draft, resumes it after logout, and activates the workspace with a valid name, locale, and time zone. The draft is private and the active workspace shows its empty state with optional modules off. | OWN-001–OWN-003, OWN-005, OWN-007 · M02.01, M02.02, M02.05, M02.06 |
| WS-02 | A valid invitation grants the intended role. The teammate can perform a granted action. Deny an out-of-role action, an expired or reused invitation, and access after revocation. Record authorized access changes. | OWN-003, SEC-002 · M02.04, M02.09, M02.10 |
| WS-03 | Switch between authorized workspaces and verify distinct settings and teams. Reject a read or write to the other workspace from an identity with only one membership. Audit privileged changes in the correct workspace. | OWN-001, Sections 16.2 and 20 · M02.02, M02.04, M02.10 |

## Review walkthrough

| Step | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| WS-01a | Attempt activation before identity verification. Verify the owner, create a private draft, then attempt activation with a required setting missing. Save valid settings and sign out. | Both activation attempts are denied. The verified owner can save the draft for later. An unrelated session cannot open it. |
| WS-01b | Sign back in as `OWNER-A`, review the saved settings, and activate Alpha before inviting teammates. | The settings are restored. Activation succeeds. The workspace home shows a genuine empty state with team and settings access for the owner. |
| WS-02a | As `OWNER-A`, invite `MEMBER-A` with a limited workspace role. Accept as `MEMBER-A`, then try to change owner settings and reuse the invitation. | Acceptance grants only the intended membership. The owner-only action and reused invitation are denied. |
| WS-02b | Try a separate expired invitation. Revoke `MEMBER-A`'s accepted membership and revisit the workspace in that teammate's existing session. | Expired acceptance grants no access. The existing session loses access to Alpha, and the owner's revocation is traceable in Alpha's audit history. |
| WS-03a | As `OWNER-A`, change Alpha's settings, switch to Beta, and try the same owner-only change. | The Alpha change succeeds and is audited in Alpha. Beta shows its own context and denies the attempted change under the member grant. |
| WS-03b | In the independent WS-03 fixture, as `MEMBER-A`, attempt to open or edit Beta's workspace settings or memberships. | The read and write are denied. Beta's settings and memberships stay unchanged. |

**Design review findings** Verification, draft creation, and activation are distinct steps under OWN-001 through OWN-003. The fixtures cover a user with different roles across two workspaces, a single-workspace member, and an empty workspace that activates before invitations.
