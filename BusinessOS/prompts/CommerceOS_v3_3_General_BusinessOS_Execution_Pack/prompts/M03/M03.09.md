## 1. EXACT feature contract — what to deliver

**Feature objective:** Inter-branch stock transfer.

**Required implementation behavior from roadmap:** Implement request/approval/pick/dispatch/in-transit/partial receipt/cancel/exception workflow; record both movement legs at agreed business transition.

**Data / API / event / screen deliverables:** Transfers/lines and transfer commands, stock in transit dashboard.

**Security / reliability / scalability focus:** Never expose source inventory after dispatch or destination availability before receipt; tenant/branch permissions checked both sides.

**Acceptance evidence:** INV-AC4 plus partial/damaged transit test passes.

**End-to-end main scenario:** Dispatch stock from branch A to branch B, track in transit and receive accepted quantity.

**Mandatory adverse/failure scenario:** Goods in transit unavailable at both until receipt; deny negative/double receipts.
